SweaxDrop 1.0.0, the first release: files, photos, folders, text and the clipboard between Windows, Android and iPhone over your own network.

**Downloads**

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `SweaxDrop-1.0.0-Windows-Setup.exe` | Windows 10 and 11. Asks once for administrator rights to add one firewall rule for `sweaxdrop.exe` (removed on uninstall). |
| `SweaxDrop-1.0.0-Android.apk` | Android 10 and newer (arm64 phones; x86_64 for emulators). Signed with the SweaxDrop release key. |
| `SweaxDrop-1.0.0-iOS-unsigned.ipa` | iPhone, iOS 15 and newer. Unsigned: sign and install it with your own Apple ID through AltStore or Sideloadly ([steps](https://github.com/Sweax3169/sweaxdrop#iphone)). An iPhone without the app can use SweaxDrop right away by scanning the QR code on the PC. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums of the files above. |

**What is in it**

- Discovery by multicast, broadcast and mDNS; add by address; QR and PIN pairing (SPAKE2, bound to both TLS keys).
- TLS 1.3 with both devices proven by their Ed25519 key; paired devices pinned.
- Up to 16 parallel lanes, 4 MiB chunks, BLAKE3 per chunk, no request per file.
- Resume from the last verified chunk after a dropped connection; pause, resume, cancel from either side; a queue per device.
- Browser client served by the PC for phones without the app.
- Windows: tray, drag and drop, Send to, notifications, start with Windows.
- Android: share target, photo picker, folders, gallery saving, a folder of your choice, foreground service.
- iPhone: Photos and Files pickers, received photos to the library, Files app folder, Bonjour discovery.
- History with speed curves; English and Turkish; light and dark; five color palettes.

Measured against LocalSend 1.18.2 on the same PC and files: see the [README](https://github.com/Sweax3169/sweaxdrop#measured-against-localsend) and [docs/benchmarks.md](https://github.com/Sweax3169/sweaxdrop/blob/main/docs/benchmarks.md).

The Windows installer is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask for confirmation; compare the file with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
